Update Help and the design notes.
1.2 WIDE SCREEN AFTER BATTLE: on wide phone screens the presentation breaks and shrinks after a battle ends. Find what the post battle layout or resize handler does, and keep the layout correct through battle end, results, level ups and return to the map. New rule: every walkable corridor and path tile, including dead ends and alternate routes, is protected. Stairs, chests, props, decor, NPCs and mimics may only go in room interiors, corners, sides and alcoves that are not on any path tile. Enforce it in generation and in the validation step (fail and regenerate if any asset is on a path tile), check every dungeon, cave, fortress, field and town, and self heal old saves on map load.
1.4 BATTLE BACK BUTTON: the back option in the battle command menu is always visible at every level of the menu.
1.5 SHOP BACK BUTTONS: every shop screen and sub screen has the back arrow next to the X.

=== PHASE 2: BATTLE AND WORLD LOGIC ===
2.1 BATTLE BACKDROPS AND GROUND: sprites sometimes stand on water, lava or in the sky. Rework battle scenes: choose the backdrop from the terrain tile type where the battle started (grass, road, forest, snow, sand, rock, cave and so on, never a hazard tile), draw every unit on a solid ground plane or platform that fits the theme, and make flying foes hover with a shadow below. Water, lava or sky only appear as scenery in the background, never under a unit. Boss and special arenas get purpose built stages. Add a test that renders every backdrop with 6 heroes and 5 foes and flags units off the ground plane.
2.2 SURPRISE ADVANTAGE: touching a roaming dungeon enemy that has not detected the party gives the party a surprise start (a free first round or a speed and damage bonus on turn one) with a "Surprise!" banner. If it detected you first, the fight starts normally. Respect the existing no auto and elite rules.
2.3 MATERIAL PLACEMENT: gathering spots must make sense. Wood only near trees, ore and stone near rocks, cliffs and walls, herbs on grass and flowers, scales and fish near water, and so on. Place each node on or beside a matching terrain feature, never on roads or random open tiles, and draw a matching prop. Add a placement test per node type.
2.4 TOWN EXITS: town exits and entrances get their own clear space. Each exit has an unobstructed approach lane at least 2 tiles wide connecting to the main paths, with no building, prop or NPC in the way, and the exit position makes sense with the town layout (north exit at the north edge, and so on). Never force the player to walk around or behind a building. Add a layout test, check every town type, and keep gate width and diagonal movement rules.
2.5 DUNGEON ENTRANCE VARIETY: vary entrance art by realm and type: holes in the ground, carved into rock faces, ruin doorways, collapsed wells, tree roots, trapdoors and so on. Always right side up, always clearly visible, and still distinct from side cave and fortress entrances.
2.6 DUAL TYPES COME LATER: dual type wayfarers (in taverns, challenges and chance encounters) and dual type regular monsters do not appear until the second realm. Stage 1 uses single types only. Bosses, minibosses, elites and special enemies may be dual type at any time. Update the tavern pool, subset draw, encounter tables and the Bestiary, and re run the stage 1 balance.
2.7 EARLY GAME HINTS: add short, skippable tutorial style hints in stage 1 that teach the systems using the party, for example "Brann prefers axes and swords. Check his sheet", "Tess is strong against flying foes", primers and detonators, weapon types, armor weights. They fire once each, can be turned off in Settings, and live in the Guides tab afterward.

=== PHASE 3: SHOPS, GEAR AND ITEMS ===
3.1 BEST FOR PARTY ORDERING: in shops, Forge and Tempering, the items that best upgrade the active party sort to the top, ordered from best to worst upgrade. When two or more options are close in value, show a short reason line explaining the tradeoff (for example "Harp: stronger buffs. Flute: cheaper, more MP"). Keep the rarity borders and the filters, and keep the craftable first rule.
3.2 SUGGEST DUPLICATES: still suggest buying or crafting an item even if it is already equipped by one party member when it would also be a good upgrade for a different party member. Show the second hero's gain.
3.3 SMITHY BEST GEAR BUTTON: add a "Buy, craft and equip best gear" button in the Smithy. It plans the best upgrade for each active party member, picking for each item the cheaper of buying or crafting when both are possible (counting gold and materials already owned), shows a full plan (what, for whom, cost, stat gains, anything it cannot afford) with a total, and only proceeds after confirmation. Support undo for the equips and a clear stop when gold runs out.
3.4 BEST PARTY AWARENESS: the Best Party button considers more than roles and elements. It checks delivery coverage (piercing, blunt, ranged, magic, healing), weapon and armor legality, and uses the Bestiary: the monsters known in the next area and what is known about the stage's boss. Even before meeting a boss, its element and archetype are known from the Guides or its theme, so use that. Show reasons per pick ("Tess: ranged answer to the flyers here"). After the player applies a Best Party, offer an immediate "Equip best gear for this party" button that runs auto equip.
3.5 TEAM BUILD HINTS: the Party and Best Party screens show what the party is missing (healer, tank, ranged answer, blunt answer, piercing answer, magic answer, primer, detonator, element coverage for the area) and name roster heroes who could fill each gap, with a one tap swap suggestion.
3.6 BOSS READINESS: each boss's info screen and the pre fight confirm show a readiness check for the current party: level against the boss, gear tier, healer, key delivery types, element answers, primer and detonator coverage, potions and revives. Give specific, actionable hints on how to improve the party. Hints never block the fight and do not spoil unknown boss mechanics.
3.7 HOLY WATER REPEAT: add a toggle on Holy Water ("Reapply automatically") that uses another one when the effect ends, if the player owns one, with a message and a stop when none are left.
3.8 ITEM LIMIT: raise the stack limit from 9 to 50 for all stackable items.
3.9 PORK CHOP SANDWICHES: the first boss victory also gives 2 Pork Chop Sandwiches with a hint explaining they take you out of a dungeon. In a dungeon, the item menu offers "Use 2: return to town" that returns the party to the last town in front of the Inn (consuming 2), next to the single use that returns to the dungeon entrance. In battle and elsewhere the earlier rules stay.
