# v0.5.3 UI pass: "Gilded Night"

The menus and HUD move from chunky oak and bronze bevels to a lacquered night-blue with thin antique-gold rules, closer to the
polish of modern pixel JRPGs. No assets, fonts, names or logos from other games: every ornament is CSS or a small SVG drawn
for this game, and the fonts are the three already embedded in `v3/fonts.css`. The page stays one self-contained file.

## Design language
- **Surfaces.** Panels are a deep ink-blue gradient (`#1b1f33` to `#111424`) with a faint diamond lattice texture and a warm
  glow at the top. Cards, rows and slots are a lighter step of the same blue. Story scrolls stay parchment, with dark ink filigree.
- **Frames.** One gold hairline outside, a dark gap, then a dimmer gold inner rule (two inset box shadows), plus SVG corner
  filigree (an L-shaped double rule with diamond ends) in all four corners. Soft drop shadows lift panels off the scene.
- **Gold means "selected" or "act here".** Primary buttons, the selected top tab, the selected chip and the active hero are gold.
  Everything else is a quiet navy slab with a gold hairline that brightens on hover and focus.
- **Command cursor.** Battle command items carry a small diamond on the left. Hover or keyboard focus lights the
  diamond, slides it in 2 px and fills the row with a gold wash. Disabled commands dim the diamond too.
- **Typography.** Jacquard 24 (blackletter) only for the logo and the Victory crest; its capitals (Q, for example) read poorly in
  longer titles, so modal titles stay in Pixelify Sans with a gilt glow. Section labels (`h3`, `h4`) are small spaced capitals
  in gold, `h4` with a diamond bullet and a fading rule. Body text is Nunito; names on cards are Nunito 800.
- **Two levels of tabs.** The top menu row is a gilt tab strip in a dark tray (selected tab filled gold); the sub row is an
  underlined bar (selected tab gold text and a gold underline). You can tell the levels apart at a glance.
- **Portraits** sit in a 2 px gilt frame with a black keyline and an inner shadow on a night-blue backdrop.
- **Bars** are smooth and glossy (a white highlight on the top half) in a black trough with a faint gold rim, instead of the old
  segmented stripes. Colours come from the same `--c` values as before (HP, MP, XP, foe HP), so meaning is unchanged.
- **Motion.** Menus fade and lift in (0.22 s), controls ease their colours (0.12 to 0.14 s). Under reduced motion (the OS setting
  or the game's own setting, `html.rm`) every new transition and entrance is off.

## Files
- `v3/ui3.css` (new): the whole pass, in one block marked "v0.5.3 UI". `v3build.py` concatenates it after `style.css` (in the
  page and in the build ID hash), so its rules win at equal specificity. The ornaments are CSS custom properties on `:root`
  (`--orn-g-*` gold corners, `--orn-i-*` ink corners for parchment, `--orn-div` divider, `--orn-tex` texture), all SVG data URIs.
  The old tokens (`--wood`, `--wood2`, `--wood3`, `--bronze`, `--ink`) are redefined as the new surfaces, so older rules that read
  them follow along.
- `v3/ui.js`: one markup tweak, the battle results panel gets the class `result` (for the centred Victory crest).
- Tests in `tools/chrome/`: `v053ua.js`, `v053ub.js`, `v053ushots.js` and `v053suite.sh` (below).

## What changed
- Title: framed scene with gilt corners and a soft vignette, a glowing logo, "(Beta)" between two divider ornaments, buttons in
  a two column grid with New journey full width on top (three columns on landscape phones), the feature list in a framed card.
- Header: a gilt hairline under it, the wallet as a dark pill, the three icon buttons 40 px square.
- World HUD: the bar under the view is a slim dark plaque (transparent on landscape phones so nothing looks cut); time and weather
  chips keep their phase colours; the location banner is a dark band between two gold rules; dialogue boxes are framed panels
  with corner filigree; the minimap gets a gold rim; the stick knob is burnished gold and A/B have a gold ring.
- Menus and shops: framed panels, the sticky title bar redrawn with its own filigree and a gilt divider (it now starts at the
  panel's top edge, so the corners never double), red close and blue back buttons as framed tiles, rows and cards as navy slabs.
- Battle: the order bar is a framed strip ("NEXT" in small capitals, the current turn in a glowing gold ring; element colours on
  each portrait border are kept), the boss bar is a dark crimson plaque with a glossy HP bar (shield pips untouched), the command
  panel is a framed panel with a sticky header and the diamond cursor, party cards are navy with the active hero in gold, foe
  cards are wine red in a red framed tray.
- Results: a centred blackletter "Victory" with a glow and a divider ornament, values in gold.
- Fixes found on the way: Tavern hero cards put their "fits" chips in the portrait column, which pushed the name to the
  far right; they now span the card. Touch targets: chips, tabs and small buttons are at least 40 px tall on touch screens.

## Kept on purpose (readability rules)
Status symbols (`stsym.js`), shield pips, the primed marker, element icons, the save indicator and cloud icon (`p5settings.js`,
`saves.js`), quest ready tags (`qready.js`) and the hint card's position (`p2world.js`; only its colours and border follow the new
look) are not restyled. The pinned battle Back button, the shop back arrows next to the X, the minimap toggle and the Graphics
setting work as before. The battle stage canvas, the world renderer and the sprites are untouched (other passes own them); only
the frames around the views changed colour, at the same border widths, so the layout maths in `worldSizeCanvas` and
`layoutBattle` see the same sizes.

## Limits
- The look comes from CSS over the existing markup; panels the JS builds with inline colours keep them (tier colours, element
  colours, guild and faction tints), which is intended.
- Tier, legendary and faction accents (`.card.hero.leg`, `.gframe`, `.tchip`) keep their own styles.
- Party menu on landscape phones (844x390): the six cards fit one view of the panel, but not at its top (the header and tabs
  take the first half); scrolling once shows them all, as before.
- Jacquard 24 is used sparingly; if a future screen wants a blackletter title, keep it to short words without Q or similar capitals.
