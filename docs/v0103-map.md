# Beta v0.10.3: Phase 4, the map

- `bigmap.js`: popup `bigmap` (ACT.worldMap on worldV3 journeys; the parchment chart is `wmChart`). `bmLayout()` places t0 at 0,0 and chains f0, t1, f1 ... by gate adjacency (`bmGate`: a town's m.gates.next/prev; a field's gate cells come from m.exits by `to.g`, its gate record holds unscaled numbers). A map that would overlap is pushed along the gate axis and joined by a dashed line. `bmBase` caches a 1 px per tile canvas per map, keyed by the explored string. Doors (dungeon, caves, fortresses) are drawn at their field entrance (`bmDoors`).
- Routes: `bmEdges(id)` lists how to leave a map (exit tile and the step through it); `bmRoute` BFS over maps, then legs {m,ex,ey,dir,to} and a final {m,tx,ty}. `bmRunLeg` plans each leg with awPlan (exit goals stand in front of the gate), the worldTick wrapper steps through on arrival (`bmEnter`: a one step plan onto the exit tile), the placeAt after hook advances to the next leg. Input, a block, a dialog or a menu drops the route (awStop wrapper); a battle keeps it (the resume in map.js keeps the plan's src, route and phase).
- `map.js` and `visuals7.js`: miniOn is on unless the setting is Off; narrow views (<560 px) get an 84x63 minimap under the area name and a round Map button (`#mmbtn`, data-act map). Icons: MAPICO_X additions (stairs, chests, vaults, keys, doors, boss, quest, questghost), MKN and MAPICO_NAME cover every drawn mark.
- Tests touched: none new. `tools/chrome/v063pb.js` expects the single Equip Best button (v0.10.2).
